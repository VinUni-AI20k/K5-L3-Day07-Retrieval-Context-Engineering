name: retrieval-audit
description: Audit a Day 7 retrieval experiment, its evidence traces, context budget and dev/test separation.
---

Read the experiment contract and relevant traces before assessing results. Check whether each claimed hit contains complete gold evidence at the correct source/page, and whether the packed context preserves it within the declared budget. Inspect at least one failure and compare it with the stated hypothesis. Verify selection used dev only and that reported numerators and denominators agree with traces. Report concrete findings with paths and distinguish unverified claims from measured results; do not change gold or tune on test.
